Derive

Browse your Google Drive faster and download whole folders directly, with no zipping and no split zips.

What it does

  • Browse My Drive, Shared with me, Recent, Starred and shared drives in List, Grid or Columns view.
  • See folder sizes and file counts, and preview pictures, video and audio.
  • Download folders into a folder you pick, with the folder structure kept.
  • Open Google Drive links in Derive (you can turn this off in settings).

How it works

Derive is a Chrome/Brave extension. You sign in with Google and give it read-only access to your Drive. It talks directly to the official Google Drive API from your browser. There is no Derive server, and your files and Drive data are never sent anywhere else.
