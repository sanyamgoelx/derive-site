Derive Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Derive is a browser extension for browsing Google Drive and downloading files and folders. This policy explains what Derive accesses and what it does with it.

The short version

  • Derive only reads your Drive. It cannot change, delete or share your files.
  • Everything happens in your browser. There is no Derive server.
  • Derive does not sell, share or transfer your data, and does not use analytics or ads.

What Derive accesses

  • Your Google Drive, read-only (the drive.readonly permission): file and folder names, sizes, dates, types, owners, thumbnails, and file contents when you preview or download a file.
  • Your Google account email, so Derive can show which account you are signed in with.

How it is used

This data is used only to show you your Drive inside Derive and to download the files you choose to your computer. Requests go straight from your browser to Google's official Drive API.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings, pinned folders, download list and cached folder listings are stored locally in your browser, using the extension's own storage.
  • Downloaded files are saved to the folder on your computer that you choose.
  • Nothing is stored on any server run by the developer.

Removing the extension deletes its local storage. You can also revoke Derive's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

Derive does not send your data to the developer or to any third party. The only service it communicates with is Google, to sign you in and read your Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Derive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
